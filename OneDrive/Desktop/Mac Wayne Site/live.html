Our Commitment to Accessibility

Mac Wayne is dedicated to making live music accessible to everyone. We partner with venues and technology providers to ensure an inclusive experience.

  • Wheelchair accessible venues
  • ASL interpretation upon request
  • Live captioning for virtual events
  • Audio descriptions for key visual elements
  • Sensory-friendly options where possible

Have specific accessibility needs? Contact us, and we'll do our best to accommodate.

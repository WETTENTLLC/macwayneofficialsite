What is Battered Coin?

Battered Coin (BTRC) is Mac Wayne's official cryptocurrency project - a revolutionary blockchain-based ecosystem that powers NFTs, exclusive digital collectibles, and accessible Web3 experiences. Built on Ethereum, BTRC is more than just a crypto token; it's the foundation of Mac Wayne's digital universe, enabling NFT ownership, exclusive digital content access, and community governance in the decentralized economy.

How to Get Battered Coin (BTRC)

  1. Set up a Crypto Wallet: You'll need a wallet that supports ERC-20 tokens (e.g., MetaMask, Trust Wallet).
  2. Acquire ETH: BTRC will be available for purchase with Ethereum (ETH) on decentralized exchanges.
  3. Connect to a DEX: Connect your wallet to a supported decentralized exchange (e.g., Uniswap).
  4. Swap ETH for BTRC: Find the BTRC token (contract address will be provided here) and swap your ETH.

Detailed instructions and official contract address will be released soon. Stay tuned!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ERC-20 token?

An ERC-20 token is a standard used for creating and issuing smart contracts on the Ethereum blockchain. Battered Coin is an ERC-20 token.

Is Battered Coin an investment?

Battered Coin is primarily a utility token designed to enhance fan engagement. While it may have value, it should not be considered a financial investment. Its value can be volatile.

The Story Behind the Film

More Than Music

"Blind & Battered" is not just a documentary about a musician; it's a testament to the human spirit's ability to overcome insurmountable challenges. It delves deep into Mac Wayne's personal life, the accident that changed everything, and his relentless pursuit of his dreams in a world not designed for him.

The film highlights his innovative contributions to music technology, making it accessible for artists with disabilities, and his advocacy for inclusivity in the entertainment industry.
